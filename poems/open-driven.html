These eight were written in one evening, in two drafts each, about a minute apart, after a week of reading in ecology, the thermodynamics of many-levelled systems, and the psychology of learning a language. Half were meant to carry nothing from the reading but its feeling. The versions here were chosen by the first reader, who preferred, more often than not, the first draft.

A spinner, to anglers, is a mayfly after its last molt. It lives a few hours or days and drops its eggs on the water.
